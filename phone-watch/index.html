Phone Watch

Every hour, a computer looks at public street webcams in 13 cities and counts how many of the people it can see are on their phones.

Talk about it

  1. Did your guess hold up? What made you pick that city?
  2. The computer only counts phones it can see. Who is it missing, and does that push the number up or down?
  3. Is "on a phone" the same as "addicted"? What would you need to see to tell the difference?
  4. Count your own street for five minutes. How close do you get to the computer?
